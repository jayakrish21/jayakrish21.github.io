RAG Knowledge Assistant

FastAPILangChainChroma/FAISSLLM

What

Context-aware assistant that answers questions from internal documents with citations.

Why

Reduce search-to-insight time and improve trust with grounded, source-linked responses.

How used

Policy/FAQ lookup, internal support, research synthesis; foundation for an enterprise knowledge hub.

Stack

FastAPI · LangChain · Chroma/FAISS · HF/OpenAI embeddings · LLM (Azure/OpenAI/Ollama) · PyPDF/Unstructured.

AI concepts

RAG pipeline (chunk → embed → retrieve) · semantic similarity · prompt augmentation · grounded generation.

Learned

Chunk/overlap tuning; embedding selection; ACL-aware retrieval; RAGAS eval; cost-aware provider routing.
